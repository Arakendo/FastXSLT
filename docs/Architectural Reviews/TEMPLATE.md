## Architectural question

State one decision-shaped question without assuming its answer.

## Trigger and evidence

Record observed facts, corpus cases, profiles, caller requirements, or defects.
Say what evidence is absent. Do not present an observation as a guarantee.

## Ownership and constraints

Identify which layer owns each semantic, policy, resource, and dependency. List
accepted ADR constraints that the alternatives must preserve.

## Alternatives

Describe viable options, including retention of the current design where
applicable. Compare correctness, complexity, performance, security, portability,
conformance, and migration consequences.

## Findings and uncertainties

State what the evidence establishes and what remains unknown.

## Disposition

Use an explicit status and outcome: accept through a named ADR, incubate, defer,
reject, or confirm no change.

## Required follow-up

- [ ] Name concrete work, owner if known, and verification evidence.

## Reopening triggers

List observable evidence that would justify another review cycle.
